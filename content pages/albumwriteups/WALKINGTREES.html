Walking Trees

 

I've never made anything that sounds like this before and I don't think I will again. it's kinda a folk album but with a bunch of electronic things thrown in. which was a complete accident. I made a bunch of folk songs and then got bored of them and then came back with different goals.

I only really like Winter Sun and Kate. Maybe my favorite things I've made. the rest of them I just cringe at though. I'm proud, kinda? but it's been slog work getting it done

Mainly the singing. I basically had to learn how to sing for this. this whole thing was a massive learning experience, and I think I've crossed a threshold, but you can also hear that in the music.

All for was really fun though. Mastaba was fun too, I plugged into a tape recorder and it just came out the way it did.

I think I'm going to go in the opposite direction now. I've been mass producing stupid pop songs, and enjoying it a lot. might be done around christmas.
